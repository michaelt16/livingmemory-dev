# Living Memory — Demo Video Script Guide (~3 min)

**Goal:** Show judges that static photos become living movies, with stories and voices that stay in the family. Emphasize Nova integration, multimodal + voice AI, and community impact.

---

## 1. HOOK & VALUE PROPOSITION (0:00–0:25)

- **Living Memory turns static photos into movies.** You don’t just scroll—you watch and listen.
- You talk to **EVA** to give the story context. We take that conversation and turn it into **narration**—in **your voice or a loved one’s voice**.
- **Judge callout:** *“We use Amazon Nova so your family’s stories and voices are preserved, not lost in a feed.”*

---

## 2. CAPTURE FLOW: PHOTOS + EVA CONVERSATION (0:25–0:55)

- Show the **photo capture** screen. You can use **placeholder images** or **import from your PC**.
- **Image context:** As you talk, we capture a **screenshot every few seconds**. That gives the system **visual context** of what you’re looking at.
- **Voice AI:** Your conversation with EVA uses **Amazon Nova 2 Sonic**—speech-to-speech—so you talk naturally; no typing required.
- Hit **Finish** → go to the **Editor**. The photo you just took is there, with the **story recap** already attached from the conversation.
- **Judge callout:** *“Nova Sonic powers the real-time conversation; the screenshot stream gives us multimodal context so EVA knows what photo you’re talking about.”*

---

## 3. EDITOR: CROP & ANIMATE (0:55–1:20)

- Use the **crop** feature—powered by **Nova** for smart framing so faces and subjects stay in frame.
- Use the **animate** feature: turn a photo into a short clip (e.g. cinematic, or style transfer like Disney/Ghibli/LEGO).
- **Judge callout:** *“Image understanding and enhancement are driven by Nova so crops and styles stay context-aware.”*

---

## 4. NARRATION: MULTIMODAL STORY (1:20–1:50)

- **Narration** isn’t generic. It uses **Nova’s multimodal understanding**: **image order**, **image content**, and the **story from your conversation**.
- We send that combined context to the model so the script **flows from the first photo to the next**—one coherent story, not isolated captions.
- If a photo has **no story yet**, you can **re-tell it** or **ask a family member** who was there; their perspective gets attached to that photo.
- **Judge callout:** *“This is Nova 2 Lite doing vision + language: it sees the sequence and the conversation and outputs narration that respects order and context.”*

---

## 5. VOICE: CLONE & SELECT (1:50–2:05)

- Show **voice cloning** (e.g. in Editor or Settings). Record a short sample → your voice is available for narration.
- In the **Editor**, you can **choose who narrates**: your cloned voice, a family member’s clone, or a **Polly** voice.
- **Judge callout:** *“So the Living Storybook doesn’t just tell the story—it tells it in Grandma’s voice or yours, so future generations hear the people who were there.”*

---

## 6. EXPORT: THREE FORMATS (2:05–2:25)

- Once the **movie arrangement** is right, **Export**.
- Show the **three export forms**:
  - **Living Storybook** (play/watch experience)
  - **Scrapbook** (flip-through, shareable)
  - **Video** (file you can keep or share anywhere)
- **Judge callout:** *“One story, multiple formats—for watching together, for printing or sharing as a book, or for keeping as a video file.”*

---

## 7. FAMILY FEED & PERSPECTIVES (2:25–2:45)

- Show the **Feed**: recent activity, new stories, new albums.
- **Questions:** If a **grandchild** (or any family member) is curious about a photo, they can **ask a question**—about that specific photo or an open question to the whole family.
- Everyone can **share their perspective**; over time we’ll **weave multiple perspectives into one story**.
- **Judge callout:** *“So it’s not one person’s version of the past—it’s the family’s, with each voice and perspective preserved.”*

---

## 8. FUTURE & IMPACT (2:45–3:00)

- **Future:** Multi-perspective weaving, richer Q&A, and more ways to keep family stories and voices in the loop.
- **Closing line:** *“Living Memory uses Amazon Nova so your photos become living stories—in your family’s voices, for the people who’ll care most later.”*
- **#AmazonNova** (say it or show it on screen).

## WOW MOMENTS TO EMPHASIZE

1. **“You talk; we turn it into a script.”** — Conversation → narration, no manual writing.
2. **“Your voice or theirs.”** — Clone and select narrator; story in Grandma’s voice.
3. **“Order and context matter.”** — Multimodal narration that follows photo order and conversation.
4. **“One story, three ways out.”** — Storybook, Scrapbook, Video from one edit.
5. **“Ask about a photo; the family answers.”** — Q&A and future multi-perspective weaving.
